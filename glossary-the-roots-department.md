# The Roots Department — Glossary

Non-trivial words from the story, with their meaning **as they are used in the story**. (The 50 target vocabulary words are not included here.)

---

**alertness** — the state of being awake and watchful. *"An alertness sixty years on duty going off shift"* — Rosa's mind finally stops guarding.

**amnesiac** — having no memory. The fig tree buds *"with amnesiac confidence"* — as if it has forgotten every bad thing that happened.

**apron** — a piece of clothing worn over clothes to keep them clean while working.

**archive** — a collection of old records and documents. *"That is the whole archive"* — all the family has is one man's memory.

**armored** — protected, as if wearing armor. Dina arrives *"armored to the chin"* — emotionally defended.

**barrier** — a fence or block that stops people from passing. At the port, it separates the family.

**borderline** — the thin line between two states. The smell of figs is on the *"borderline"* between ripeness and rot.

**catastrophe** — a complete disaster. Used half-jokingly for Maya's ugly first bowl.

**cellar** — a cool underground room for storing things. The clay is *"cool as a cellar."*

**centering** — placing the clay exactly in the middle of the spinning wheel. In the story it is also a life lesson: stay steady, be the center.

**colander** — a kitchen bowl full of holes, used to drain water from food. Rosa's word for her own leaking memory: *"everything else is a colander."*

**collapse** — to fall down suddenly. What badly centered clay does on the wheel.

**coordinates** — the exact numbers that give a position on a map. Rosa is *"lost somewhere without coordinates"* — completely lost.

**(war) correspondent** — a reporter who describes a war. Rosa tells neighborhood news in this dramatic voice.

**corridor** — a long hallway, especially in a hospital.

**crooked** — not straight; leaning. The most important word in the story: *"It's crooked. So is everything that survives."*

**cumin** — a warm-tasting spice used in soup.

**curriculum** — the planned content of lessons. On the wheel, *"failure was the curriculum"* — failing is how you learn.

**defenses** — emotional protections. Rosa speaks once in *"a voice without any defenses in it."*

**delighted** — very pleased.

**diplomatic** — polite and careful, avoiding open conflict. Rosa pushes the laptop away half a centimeter — *"a diplomatic expulsion."*

**dock** — the platform at a port where boats load and unload people.

**drought** — a long period without rain.

**emigrated** — left one's country to live in another. A joke about memory: *"the date had emigrated for good."*

**empire** — a great kingdom. Used playfully for the mint and the toad's territory.

**expulsion** — the act of pushing someone or something out.

**fever** — a high body temperature when you are sick.

**fluorescent** — a kind of flat, bright white electric light, typical of offices and hospitals.

**fortress** — a strong building made to defend a city.

**funneled** — narrowed down and passed through, like liquid through a funnel. The ancestors' faces *"funneled down the centuries"* into Maya.

**fury** — strong, burning anger.

**generous** — kind and giving. *"A bowl by a generous court"* — only a kind judge would call it a bowl.

**glaze** — the glassy coating baked onto pottery. *"A good glaze hides nothing."*

**grinding** — working slowly, steadily, without stopping. The algorithm *"grinding patiently"* through millions of DNA samples.

**gulls** — white seabirds found around harbors.

**harbor** — a protected place by the sea where boats stay.

**hum** — a low, continuous sound, like the turning wheel.

**immovable** — impossible to move; refusing to change. Rosa's voice when she shuts a subject down.

**insolent** — boldly disrespectful. The mint grows back *"insolent"* — as if it never asked permission.

**kiln** — a very hot oven used to fire (harden) pottery.

**knuckle** — the joint in the middle of a finger.

**latitude** — a position on the map (north–south). The laptop *"knew the latitude of all of this and none of it"* — it knows the location, not the meaning.

**listing** — leaning to one side (said about boats). Maya's bowl has *"a lean like a listing boat."*

**load-bearing** — holding up the weight of a building. Said about a wall you must not remove. *"The silence was load-bearing"* — the family stood on it.

**lurch** — a sudden, unsteady movement, like a drunk person.

**luxury** — something pleasant that you can live without. *"Ancestors are a luxury. We left in a hurry."*

**masterpiece** — an artist's best, most perfect work.

**mineral** — like stone or earth. The *"mineral smell of clay."*

**murmur** — soft, low, unclear speech, like talking in sleep.

**neurologist** — a doctor who treats the brain and memory.

**numb** — without feeling. Maya's arm goes numb because she refuses to move it.

**pamphlet** — a thin information booklet, like the one doctors give after a diagnosis.

**parliament** — a group that meets to discuss and decide. A joke: the wasps hold *"their slow parliament"* over the fallen figs.

**patient** — two meanings in the story: (1) a person being treated by a doctor; (2) calm, able to wait without anger.

**peninsula** — land almost completely surrounded by water (the *"Iberian Peninsula"* = Spain and Portugal).

**pinch** — to press something between finger and thumb. How you remove tomato *suckers*.

**plateau** — a flat period when an illness stops getting worse. The story's most hopeful medical word: *"some hold a plateau for years."*

**pothole** — a hole in a road. Maya is angry that her grandmother is filed under the same word ("hazard") as a pothole.

**qualifiers** — small words that soften or limit a statement, like *early* and *mild* after a diagnosis.

**recite** — to say aloud from memory. Rosa recites pottery rules *"like scripture."*

**refund** — money given back for something you bought.

**resident** — a person (or toad) who lives in a place.

**retired** — stopped working because of age. Rosa says her hands *"retired before the rest of her."*

**revelation** — a moment when a hidden truth is shown. The website's slow opening of the map is *"a little theater of revelation."*

**revolution** — one full turn of a wheel. (Also fits the bigger meaning: a complete change.)

**rib (tool)** — a flat tool used to smooth and straighten the wall of a pot.

**rim** — the top edge of a bowl or cup.

**rounding error** — a tiny difference that comes from rounding numbers; something so small it can be ignored. *"A rounding error of history."*

**scandal** — a shocking event. Rosa uses it as a compliment: *"The crop is a scandal"* — shockingly good.

**scar** — the mark left on skin after a wound heals. Every pottery rule is *"the scar of ten thousand broken pots."*

**scripture** — holy writings. Rosa recites her rules like holy text.

**sepia** — the brown-yellow color of very old photographs.

**settle / settled** — to calm down and come to rest. Something behind Rosa's face *"moved like water under ice, and settled."*

**smug** — too pleased with yourself. The glowing dot on the map looks *"smug."*

**snort** — a rough sound through the nose showing you don't respect something.

**sponge** — a soft object that holds water. Little Estreya's job at the wheel: *"the minister of water."*

**stamp** — the official tool (and mark) used to approve papers. *"A man at a table with a stamp deciding the future of the world one family at a time."*

**statistics** — numbers that measure performance, like call times.

**stubborn** — refusing to change or give up.

**suckers** — small unwanted shoots that grow on a tomato plant and must be pinched off.

**supervise** — to watch over work and give directions. Rosa supervises from her chair.

**surrender** — to give up, to stop fighting. *"The mint surrendered first."*

**synagogue** — a Jewish house of prayer. Its record books were destroyed with the city.

**synthetic** — artificial, made by machines, not natural. The *"synthetic strings"* of hold music.

**tenderness** — soft, gentle love.

**treaty** — a formal agreement to stop fighting. Rosa and Dina stop arguing *"by unspoken treaty."*

**undignified** — embarrassing; without honor. Spitting into the tube is *"an undignified process."*

**urgent** — needing action right now.

**vault** — the heavy safe room of a bank. The clinic's doors *"still wearing the heavy doors of vaults."*

**veteran** — a person with long experience.

**visa** — official permission to enter a country. A metaphor: in the country of clay-talk, *"both of them had visas."*

**wedge (clay)** — to press and fold clay again and again to push out air bubbles.

**weightlessness** — the state of having no weight. The percentages have *"the strange weightlessness of a fact about someone else."*

**wicker** — chair material made of woven dry branches.

**wobble** — to shake unsteadily from side to side.

**workshop** — a place where things are made by hand. Rosa's father's pottery workshop, two streets above the harbor.

## The old Spanish (Ladino) phrases

The family's old language, carried out of Spain five hundred years ago:

- **Estreya** — "star."
- **Dame la mano** — "Give me your hand."
- **Ya viene el vapor** — "The boat (steamship) is coming." *Vapor* is the old word for a steamship.
- **Aquí está la mano** — "Here is the hand."
